How to Play

Cast & roll: one tap casts a hexagram (1–64, Fate) and rolls your die (Agency).

Pass or fail compares against your Personal DC. DCs 1–16 and 33–48 are Roll-Over; 17–32 and 49–64 are Roll-Under. Your die and modifier always push toward success: Over needs Cast + Die + Mod ≥ DC; Under needs Cast ≤ DC + Die + Mod.

The Ovine Pull: near a band edge (DC 15–18, 31–34, 47–50) instinct may drag the check into the neighbouring mode.

The Bolt: a natural 1 turns your agency against you at full die size.

Tier (Triumph / Conflict / Disaster) comes from the die alone and sets how it feels and what it costs.

Fate Points reveal hexagram lines and avert Disaster. Every 2 spent adds 1 Skulduggery.
