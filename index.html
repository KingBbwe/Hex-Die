How to Play: One-Click Hex & Die App

Weave Fate and Choice in Auld Ewe’s Cosmos

Cast & Roll (One Tap):

Tap the Harmonize button to simultaneously:

  • Cast a Hexagram: The app generates one of 64 I-Ching archetypes (e.g., Hexagram 29: The Abysmal for danger).
  • Roll Your Die: Automatically uses your selected die type (d6 to d100).

Add Modifiers (Optional):

Input bonuses/penalties. The app calculates your Total Value (Hexagram + Die Roll + Modifier).

Interpret the Cosmic Threads:

Triumph/Conflict/Disaster: Determined by your die roll.

DC Met/Exceeded? The hexagram’s lines shape consequences (e.g., Line 5 = peak success, Line 6 = cosmic irony).

Example Playthrough:

Tap Harmonize → Casts Hexagram 29 (The Abysmal) + Rolls d20 (Triumph: 18) + Modifier +2 = Total 49 (DC 29 Exceeded).

Outcome: Leap the chasm with feral grace (Line 5), but trigger a rockslide (Line 6).
